# EVNOI — concept storefront

An independent EquiNova case-study website for EVNOI with one consistent brand hero and three audience stories: clear thinking, new parents and breakfast planning.

## Preview locally

From this directory, run `python3 -m http.server 8080` and open `http://localhost:8080`.

## GitHub Pages publishing

This repository includes a `gh-pages` branch containing the static site. To make it public, the repository owner must open **Settings → Pages → Build and deployment**, choose **Deploy from a branch**, select **gh-pages** and **/(root)**, then save. GitHub should publish to `https://raunakpandeybiz.github.io/evnoi/` once the deployment completes. Do not consider that URL live until it returns the website rather than a 404 page.

The website uses relative asset paths, so it works beneath the `/evnoi/` project path.

## What is interactive

- On desktop all three audience cards fit in one row, with an automatically advancing spotlight. On compact screens they auto-slide one full card at a time. Arrow controls are available; motion pauses briefly after touch/focus and is disabled for reduced-motion preferences.
- Scroll-controlled 3D journal preview: choose the 30-Day Reset or Little Beginnings, then move down the page to open the cover and advance through three spreads. An upright cover panel stays visible while the pages turn; scrolling back reverses the effect without a large empty scroll runway.
- Three selectable corporate collaboration bundles
- Product search and filters, detail dialogs, favorites, responsive navigation and a demo bag

## Product concept

The 30-Day Reset is a proposed thin 30-page journal with a reusable cover held by two brass screw posts. A monthly refill can be inserted rather than replacing the cover. The **illustrative** pricing on the site is ₹599 for a starter cover with pages and ₹149 for a 30-page refill pack. These figures are hypotheses, not confirmed EVNOI prices or validated unit economics. The proposed keepsake and breakfast collections are also concepts.

## Important disclosure

This is **not EVNOI's official store**. Editorial images are AI-generated concept imagery, and the precision-typography journal covers are original SVG mockups. Product designs, kits, specifications and prices require EVNOI's approval and manufacturing validation. The bag is a demo; no payment or orders are processed. The newsletter form does not store or send emails. Confirm the actual catalog and use approved brand assets before publishing as an official store.
