# EVNOI — concept storefront

An independent EquiNova case-study website for EVNOI with one consistent brand hero and three scrollable audience stories: clear thinking, new parents, and breakfast planning.

## Preview

From this directory, run `python3 -m http.server 8080` and open `http://localhost:8080`.

## What is interactive

- Scrollable story cards with previous/next arrows, native touch scrolling and scroll snapping
- 3D open/close journal: choose between the 30-Day Reset and Little Beginnings and turn through three sample pages
- Selectable corporate collaboration bundles
- Product search and category filters, detail dialogs, favorites, responsive navigation and a demo bag with quantity controls
- Motion on scroll and hover, with reduced-motion accessibility support

## Product concept

The 30-Day Reset is a proposed thin 30-page journal with a reusable cover held by two brass screw posts. A monthly refill can be inserted rather than replacing the cover. The **illustrative** pricing on the site is ₹599 for a starter cover with pages and ₹149 for a 30-page refill pack. These figures are hypotheses, not confirmed EVNOI prices or validated unit economics. The proposed keepsake and breakfast collections are also concepts.

## Important disclosure

This is **not EVNOI's official store**. Editorial images are AI-generated concept imagery, and the precision-typography journal covers are original SVG mockups. Product designs, kits, specifications and prices require EVNOI's approval and manufacturing validation. The bag is a demo; no payment or orders are processed. The newsletter form does not store or send emails. Confirm the actual catalog and use approved brand assets before publishing as an official store.
